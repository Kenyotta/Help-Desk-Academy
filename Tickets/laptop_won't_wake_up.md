\# Executive Summary



A Customer Support employee reported that her laptop displayed a black screen while the power indicator slowly blinked. The laptop was plugged in, but pressing the power button did not wake the system. The user was instructed to disconnect all external peripherals and perform a hard reset by holding the power button for approximately 30 seconds until all indicator lights and fan activity stopped. After powering the laptop back on, the system booted successfully and normal operation was restored.

\# Investigation Timeline



\## Step 1 – Review Ticket



Reviewed the symptoms.



Observed:



\- Black screen.

\- Slowly blinking power indicator.

\- No response to normal power button press.



\---



\## Step 2 – Gather Additional Information



Confirmed:



\- Laptop remained plugged into power.

\- User had already attempted pressing the power button multiple times.

\- No visible display output.



\---



\## Step 3 – Corrective Action



Instructed the user to:



1\. Disconnect all external peripherals and accessories.

2\. Hold the power button for approximately 30 seconds until all lights and fan activity stopped.

3\. Release the power button.

4\. Press the power button once to start the laptop normally.



\---



\## Step 4 – Verification



Confirmed:



\- Laptop powered on successfully.

\- Display returned.

\- Windows loaded normally.

\- User verified the laptop was operational.



Ticket resolved.



\---



\# Root Cause



The laptop became stuck in a low-power or suspended hardware state and did not respond to normal wake attempts.



A hard reset cleared the residual power state and allowed the system to initialize correctly.



\---



\# Resolution



\- Verified reported symptoms.

\- Instructed user to disconnect external devices.

\- Performed hard reset by holding the power button for 30 seconds.

\- Restarted the laptop.

\- Verified successful boot.

\- Confirmed normal operation with the user.



\---



\---



\# Lessons Learned



\- A blinking power LED with a black screen may indicate the system is stuck in a suspended power state.

\- Disconnecting peripherals before performing a hard reset helps eliminate external hardware as a contributing factor.

\- A hard reset is often an effective first step before escalating a laptop that will not wake from sleep.
